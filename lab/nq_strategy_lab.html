Strategy

Risk exits

0 turns a stop or target off. Checked against each bar's high and low; if both hit in one bar, the stop counts. After a stop or target, the strategy waits for its signal to change before re-entering.

Account rules

0 turns a rule off. Losses count open trades and costs. The daily limit closes everything and pauses trading until the next session at 18:00 NY. The max drawdown trails the highest closing equity; breaching it ends trading for the rest of the test.

Account & costs

Fills at the close of the bar that gives the signal. A tick is 0.25 pt.

Coin-flip test

Could luck have done as well? The test re-runs this strategy's trades many times with a coin making the decision, through the same costs, account rules, trading windows and no-trade days, and counts how often the coin matches or beats the strategy.

What the lab can run

Paste a TradingView strategy() script (Pine v5 or v6). Supported: input.* (they become settings), variables, var, if/else, switch, for/while, your own functions, tuples, history x[1], ta.* (sma, ema, rma, wma, hma, vwma, alma, rsi, atr, stdev, highest, lowest, crossover, crossunder, change, macd, bb, kc, stoch, cci, dmi, supertrend, vwap, pivothigh/low, valuewhen, barssince, linreg and more), math.*, time() sessions, hour/minute, and strategy.entry/exit/close/close_all/cancel with stops, limits, profit/loss ticks, trailing stops, partial exits and pyramiding.

Not supported: arrays, matrices, maps, drawings (they're skipped), and request.security() on another timeframe. Orders placed on a bar fill at the next bar's open, like TradingView. Hours use exchange time (Chicago) unless you pass a time zone. Position size, commission, slippage and capital come from the lab's settings unless the script passes qty.
